FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is DiskDoom?
A native Mac app for exploring storage, finding large files and duplicates, and reviewing cleanup opportunities — with visual maps that make disk usage easy to understand.
Does the website scan my Mac?
No. This website only shows the product. Scanning happens in the installed Mac application, on your machine.
Does cleanup permanently delete files?
No. Standard file cleanup moves permitted items to the macOS Trash after you review and confirm your selection. Emptying the Trash is a separate action you take yourself.
Why does DiskDoom request Full Disk Access?
Some locations are protected by macOS. Full Disk Access determines what the app can inspect; without it, scans can be incomplete — and DiskDoom will tell you what was skipped rather than guess.
Why might storage totals differ from Finder or macOS Settings?
Scan scope, permissions, measurement timing, and filesystem accounting can produce different totals. A displayed size is what DiskDoom measured for the indexed files — it is not a promise of immediately recoverable space.
Can it scan external drives?
You can choose scan targets, including external volumes your Mac has mounted. Support can vary by filesystem — network volumes may be limited.
Can it remove duplicate photos from Apple Photos?
No. Duplicate cleanup inside a Photos library is not supported — library-managed cleanup belongs in the Photos app. DiskDoom’s duplicate detection covers byte-identical files found by scanning.
Are forecasts and compression savings guaranteed?
No. Forecasts are projections based on the usage history DiskDoom has collected — more history means a more grounded estimate. Compression savings depend on the selected files and filesystem behavior, and are shown as estimates.
Does DiskDoom work offline?
Scanning and exploration run locally on your Mac. Only license activation and validation contact the license server. If the server is unreachable, an offline grace period keeps licensed features working.
How long is the trial, and what happens afterward?
The trial runs 14 days with full features. After it ends, you can still activate with a license key — otherwise browsing remains available in read-only mode until you activate.
Which Macs are supported?
DiskDoom is a native macOS app. The current build targets macOS 13 and later; any different requirements for optional features will be stated on the download page.
How do I activate or move a license?
Activate with your license key and purchase email inside the app. To move to another Mac, use “Deactivate This Mac” first — that frees the activation slot. Your license details and device token are stored in the macOS Keychain, never in plain files.
